Privacy policy

This policy explains what data odisse.com.br collects, why, and how you can decide about it. The site is run by Odisse — Architecture & Design Studio, based in João Pessoa, Brazil.

What we record without cookies

To know how many people visit the site and what they look at, we record anonymously: the page opened, where the visit came from (for example, Instagram or Google), the type of device, the language, the approximate city of the connection and clicks on contact links (WhatsApp, e-mail, phone). Each visitor is recognised only within the same day, through a code that cannot identify them. IP addresses are not stored. These records are deleted automatically within 400 days.

Google cookies (only with your consent)

If you accept in the cookie notice, the site turns on Google Analytics and Google Ads, which use cookies to measure visits and ad results. This data is handled by Google under the Google privacy policy. If you decline, none of this is loaded. You can change your choice at any time with the button at the end of this page.

What stays in your browser

The site keeps a few preferences in your own browser: the language, how you view the projects (list or gallery), your cookie choice and a copy of the project list, so pages load faster. This information is not sent to us.

When you contact us

If you write to us by e-mail, WhatsApp or phone, we use your contact details only to reply and handle your request. We do not sell or pass on this data.

Where the site runs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its content and statistics are stored on Cloudflare. These services may log technical connection data, such as IP addresses, for security and operation.

Your rights

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018), you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, and ask questions about this policy, by writing to contato@odisse.com.br.
